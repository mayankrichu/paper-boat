FIND YOUR FLOW

A few gentle pointers.

You’re the captain.

Arrow keys or WASD to steer. On touchscreens, drag anywhere on the river or use the arrow buttons.

A little stardust.

Every star earns 25 points. Every meter earns 1. Follow the trails through the clear water.

Paper is precious.

Avoid rocks and logs. You have 3 hearts; floating hearts restore one. Ducks and lilies are just scenery.

Safe & sound.

A shield keeps you safe for 7 seconds. Just drift mode has no damage or competitive records.

Rain after level 4.

Rain, waves and splashes fill the wetness meter and make the boat sit lower in the water. Thunder can scorch the paper; catch tape rolls to patch burn marks.
